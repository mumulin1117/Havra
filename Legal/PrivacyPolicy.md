# Havra Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Havra is a community app for sharing everyday life across Southeast Asia, including local routines, city scenes, celebrations, food, fashion, family moments, and other cultural details. This Privacy Policy explains how Havra collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app.

If you have questions or requests about privacy, contact us at: Harvrauser@gmail.com

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate Havra, keep the community safe, and support the features you choose to use.

### Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

- Username, display name, profile photo, bio, and profile settings.
- Email address, phone number, Apple ID relay email, or other sign-in information if you choose to provide it.
- Authentication identifiers needed to keep your account secure.
- Account status, preferences, blocked users, and moderation history.

### Content You Provide

Havra is built around community posts and conversation. We may collect and store:

- Photos, videos, captions, comments, reactions, bookmarks, messages, and other content you publish or send.
- Metadata connected to your posts, such as upload time, content type, engagement counts, and moderation status.
- Reports, appeals, and safety-related explanations you submit.

Published posts, public profile information, public comments, and visible reactions may be seen by other users. Private messages, if available, are not public, but may be reviewed when necessary for safety, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or user support.

### Purchase Information

Video posting is an optional feature available through in-app purchase. Viewing published videos does not require a separate purchase.

If you make an in-app purchase, Apple processes the payment. Havra may receive purchase confirmation details from Apple, such as product identifier, transaction identifier, entitlement status, and purchase history needed to unlock, restore, or support the purchased feature. Havra does not receive your full payment card number from Apple.

### Device, App, and Usage Information

We may collect:

- Device model, operating system version, app version, language, region, and basic diagnostic information.
- Log data such as app launches, feature use, crashes, errors, and security events.
- Approximate location inferred from device settings, IP address, or content context, where permitted and needed for safety, localization, or service operation.

### Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Havra may request access to:

- Photos or media library, so you can choose photos or videos to post.
- Camera or microphone, so you can capture photos, videos, or audio inside the app.
- Notifications, so you can receive activity, safety, or account alerts.

You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature may not work.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create, maintain, and secure your account.
- Display posts, comments, reactions, profiles, messages, and community features.
- Enable optional paid video posting and restore eligible purchases.
- Personalize feeds, recommendations, language, and community experience.
- Provide reporting, blocking, moderation, and safety tools.
- Detect spam, fraud, harmful behavior, impersonation, harassment, and policy violations.
- Respond to support requests, reports, appeals, and legal obligations.
- Improve app performance, reliability, design, and community quality.

We do not use sensitive personal information to decide whether a user can participate in the community, except where necessary for safety, legal compliance, or enforcement of the Terms of Service.

## 3. Community Safety and Moderation

Havra is intended to remain open, respectful, and centered on real Southeast Asian life. To support that purpose, we may review public content, reports, account behavior, and relevant messages when necessary to:

- Remove harmful, illegal, abusive, exploitative, misleading, or spam content.
- Limit or remove accounts that repeatedly violate our rules.
- Respond to reports involving harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, child safety, self-harm, violence, or illegal activity.
- Prevent unwanted contact through blocking, reporting, and account restrictions.

Moderation decisions may use human review, automated tools, or both. Reports and enforcement records may be retained for longer than ordinary account data when needed to protect users, prevent repeat abuse, document decisions, or comply with law.

## 4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information in the following limited situations:

- With other users, when you publish public profile information, posts, comments, reactions, or other visible activity.
- With service providers that help operate hosting, storage, analytics, crash reporting, moderation, support, security, or messaging systems.
- With Apple, as needed for in-app purchases, app distribution, diagnostics, and platform services.
- With law enforcement, regulators, courts, or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect users, rights, safety, or the integrity of Havra.
- During a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or transfer of assets, subject to reasonable privacy protections.

Service providers may use information only to provide services to Havra and must protect it appropriately.

## 5. User Controls

You can:

- Edit profile information where the app provides profile settings.
- Delete posts, comments, or media where deletion controls are available.
- Block users to limit unwanted contact.
- Report content, users, or messages that may violate community rules.
- Change device permissions through iOS Settings.
- Request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy help by contacting Harvrauser@gmail.com.

## 6. Account Deletion

If Havra allows account creation, Havra will provide an in-app way to request account deletion. You may also contact Harvrauser@gmail.com for help.

When your account is deleted, we will delete or de-identify personal information associated with your account unless we need to retain certain information for legal, safety, security, fraud prevention, purchase restoration, dispute resolution, or legitimate operational reasons. Public content already copied, quoted, cached, backed up, or shared by other users may not disappear immediately from every location.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including account operation, community features, safety, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and business records.

Examples:

- Account information is generally kept while your account remains active.
- Public posts and comments remain available until you delete them, your account is deleted, or we remove them under our rules.
- Reports, moderation records, security logs, and abuse-prevention data may be retained for a longer period to protect the community.
- Purchase entitlement records may be retained as needed to restore purchases and resolve payment or access issues.

## 8. Children and Young Users

Havra is not intended for children under 13. If local law requires a higher minimum age or parental consent, users must meet that requirement before using Havra.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Havra, contact Harvrauser@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

## 9. International Use

Havra may be used by people in different countries and communities. Your information may be processed or stored in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative measures to protect information. No app, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please keep your account credentials private and report suspicious activity.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Havra changes or as legal requirements evolve. If changes are material, we will provide notice through the app, App Store listing, or another reasonable method. The updated version will show a new “Last updated” date.
