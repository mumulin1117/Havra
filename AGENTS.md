* **App Store 描述：

Morning markets.  Ferry rides, family tables, neighborhood games, festival lights, and ordinary days that look different from one city to the next.

Havra brings these moments together in a community shaped by everyday life across Southeast Asia.

Open the feed and move between local routines, city scenes, celebrations, food, fashion, family moments, and small cultural details. React to posts, leave thoughtful comments, and see how daily life changes across different places and communities.

Video posting is an optional feature available through in-app purchase. It gives users another way to publish street scenes, event clips, daily routines, community moments, and longer visual stories. Viewing published videos does not require a separate purchase.

Reporting, blocking, and moderation tools are available throughout the community to address harmful content and unwanted contact, helping Havra remain open, respectful, and centered on real Southeast Asian life.
